Cooling-Off Period

New members have 48 hours from the date of signing up to the booking system, or as agreed with VFIT, to cancel their membership without penalty. Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing within this period. If a session has already taken place within the 48-hour window, that session will be charged at the applicable casual rate.

In the event of a coach-initiated cancellation, sessions will be rescheduled at no penalty to the member.
